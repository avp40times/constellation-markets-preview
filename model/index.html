What this changes in the model. XTB is the clearest warning for the "buy growth" lever: as its client base grew 24-fold, revenue per active client collapsed, not just acquisition cost. I've added a new slider for that effect. Its default of 20% is well under the 76% XTB saw, because that figure also reflects six years of product mix shift (XTB added stocks, ETPs and lower-margin instruments), not acquisition speed alone.

What this doesn't settle. CMC's 80% "client income retained" is a different metric to this model's A-book cost or B-book result sliders, and one data point from 2022 is not a trend, so I haven't used it to move either. IG's revenue-per-client figure is my own division of two reported totals, not a number IG publishes, and its one-year comparison doesn't show whether a 28-month average client life is too short or too long for a more institutional client base. A true comparison would need each broker's own cohort or tenure data, which none of these disclosures give.

Sources: XTB FY2025 results, XTB per-client trend, 2019–2025, CMC Markets Q1 FY2022, IG Group Q1 FY2025.

H1 figures are trading income less customer income. As a share of revenue, the house result is small and customer income is nearly all of it. So the split only makes sense as a share of variability. Limits of this draft

  • One blended client. No client tiers, regions or IB-introduced clients as separate groups.
  • Shock sizes are my assumptions, not fitted to a real broker's history. Book data from a target would replace them.
  • Average life of 28 months is Plus500's FY2025 active clients ÷ new clients (242,000 ÷ 104,500). Its Q1 2025 figures give about 15 months. None of XTB, CMC or IG publish a cohort table either, so this stays an estimate.
  • The new ARPU-decline-on-growth slider is calibrated to one broker's six-year trend, which bundles acquisition pace with product mix change. Treat it as illustrative, not fitted.
  • Exit multiple and purchase price are placeholders, because the deck gives neither.
  • The management revenue share starts at 0% because the deck gives no rate. Move the slider to see what it costs.
  • Single currency and one acquisition. The second deal the roadmap expects in year two is not modelled.
  • The roadmap's headcount path sets the pace of cost savings. The migration loss is one event in one month.
